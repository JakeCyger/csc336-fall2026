Summer PC Build

Overview

When I was 13 years old, I bought my first gaming PC. 6 years later, I decided to build my new one and ship it to DC! I worked with one of my friend's dad as I had no experience building any electronics but he did. Little did I know, he had not built a PC since 2001 and ended up hurting my process a bit. But, after a long week, my PC was finally completed!


Components

  • CPU: AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D
  • GPU: NVIDIA RTX 4080
  • RAM: Corsair 32GB DDR5
  • Motherboard: Gigabyte B850 EAGLE WIFI6E ATX AM5
  • Storage: Silicon Power UD90 2 TB
  • PSU: MSI MAG A750GL PCIE5
  • CPU Cooler: Thermalright Peerless Assassin
  • Case: MONTECH XR Panoramic High Airflow

My Experience

Because I recruited the help of my friend's father, I would meet him at his office everyday after he finished work. However, he finished work at 4 and I didn't start work until 2. So I would get off work around 6:30-7 and spend the rest of my day, in my work clothes, in his office building this PC. He was no longer proficient in the field, which made the process more challenging. After around 2-3 days, we finally got it to boot and I went home to download the windows booter onto a USB drive. When I got back to his office, he instructed me in the wrong way which cleared the USB. That meant that I needed to go home and do the same process again. When I finally made it back, he had bad news for me. While I was gone, he accidentally swung a cord into the motherboard which fried my PSU, CPU, and motherboard dissapointing to say the least. We had to replace those parts and after 4 more tedious days and various other setbacks, we had completed it. The final build was all done by me while he was on a business call. I felt accomplished.
